PEDAGOGICAL CORE: Ampere ke purane niyam (∮ B • dl = μ₀ Ic) ke mutabiq capacitor plates ke beech koi physical conduction current nahi tha (Ic = 0), isliye wahan magnetic field zero honi chahiye thi. Lekin Maxwell ne discover kiya ki plates ke beech samay ke sath badalta hua electric field (dE/dt ≠ 0) ek Displacement Current (Id) produce karta hai, jiska maan theek wire ke conduction current (Ic) ke barabar hota hai aur circuit mein continuity maintain rehti hai.
